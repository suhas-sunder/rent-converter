What Does PCM Mean in Rent?
PCM means per calendar month. A rent listing of GBP 1,200 PCM means GBP 1,200 for one
calendar month, not one 4-week period.
PCM means per calendar month
Per calendar month rent uses calendar months, which vary from 28 to 31 days. It is
different from weekly rent and from an every-4-weeks cycle, which repeats every 28 days.
365-day comparison: weekly rent x 365 / 7 / 12
PCM describes a rent period. It does not interpret lease terms, fees, included costs, or legal obligations.

Compare weekly and PCM conventions
PW (per week) and PCW (per calendar week) both describe a seven-day week here. PCM means
per calendar month. Check which conversion your agreement uses: a 52-week budget and a
365-day equivalent give slightly different answers.

Four weeks would be £2,000.00 for 28 days. That is a different payment period from PCM.
Both directions use matching fractions; reversing a rounded display may differ by a few pence. Calculations
retain decimal precision until display rounding. A 52-week budget covers 364 days; the daily-rate model
spreads 365 days over 12 months, including in leap years. Neither counts the actual payments in a particular
year, nor determines what your contract requires. GBP is a currency label, with no exchange conversion.
